Strapontin · maquettes

Static, clickable mockups. Each frame is a live page: click through, open sheets, follow links. Fake data, real venues. Open a page directly to see it full screen.

1. Chercher ou ajouter

One field for everything: typing searches the catalogue live and the Toulouse agendas on demand; pasting a link imports the page. The draft review ends with one tap that saves both the production and the outing.

2. Spectacle

The shared record and the member's private layer on one page: your outings first, then the run, the cast (favourites starred, with how many of their shows you've seen), other stagings of the same text. "Modifier" reuses the draft review form.

3. À voir and Journal

Two tabs, À voir first because it is the one you act on. À voir is ordered by urgency: wishes by the end of their run, finished runs last with a one-tap "remove / seen". The journal groups seen outings by month with rating and the start of the comment.

4. Personne and Moi

A person page lists productions by role, with your own status on each, a favourite toggle and a private note. "Moi" gathers favourites (the only place they are listed), the invite, data export and the admin entry.
